# Review against the v2 brief (2026-11-09)

The current game is playable, but it is not a complete implementation of every requirement in `prompt-bieltructor-v2.md`. Passing automated checks does not establish 100% reliability or the requested frame rates on physical devices.

## Phase comparison

| Phase | Implemented | Remaining or simplified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Worlds | Six worlds, Creative/Adventure, four difficulties, world settings, rename, duplicate, export/import | World thumbnails; exact requested 96/128 dimensions (current islands are 97/129); tool-wear rules |
| 2. Time/weather | Day/night, sky, stars, clock, sleep, rain/snow/storms | Flood-filled block light levels; rain extinguishing outdoor campfires |
| 3. Terrain | Deterministic chunks, six biomes, caves, ores, ruins/cabins | Flowing water, underground lakes, lava/magma, copper, wells |
| 4. Items/crafting | 36 inventory slots, chests, recipes, furnaces, farming, fishing, bow | Full tool families and durability; inventory drag/drop and stack splitting; bucket/shears interactions; missing decorative shapes and coloured variants |
| 5. Animals | Chickens, cows, sheep, pigs, rabbits, dogs, cats, horses, birds; breeding/taming/riding | Bees/honey; swimming fish entities; sheep dyeing; requested biome-specific distribution; animal-friends switch (animals are already friendly) |
| 6. Monsters | Six original types, difficulty and light checks, combat, drops | Exact light-level spawning depends on block lighting; some rewards differ from the brief |
| 7. Survival | Health, hunger, oxygen, falls, melee/bow, respawn and recovery bags | Charged bow and shield; lava damage depends on lava implementation |
| 8. HUD | Vitals, hotbar, clock, compass, maps, large text, tips | Icons-only reading mode; complete requested map marker set |
| 9. Guidance | Guided tutorial and twelve shared stickers | Some goals are simplified (shelter counts placed blocks); dog naming is not required for the taming sticker |
| 10. Audio | Synthesized effects and ambient music, separate volumes | Complete material-specific footsteps and all requested animal/environment sounds |
| 11. Parents | Written arithmetic gate, reminders, difficulty cap, monsters off | Physical-device usability verification with a parent |
| 12. Quality | Delta saves, v1 migration, validation, incremental chunks, quality profiles, debug stats, automated tests | Block lighting tests, comprehensive particle pooling, physical-device performance measurements; main.js remains large |
| 13. Platforms | Shared JS, native Mac/iOS wrappers, touch/keyboard/controller mappings, lifecycle saving, optional cloud bridge, release scripts | Fully unified action layer; physical controllers and devices; real iCloud conflicts/coordination across devices; signed archives/notarization |

These gaps are not removed requirements. They are outstanding work relative to the original brief.

## Fixes in this review

- npm package metadata now matches the native 2.0.0 version.
- Automatic cloud synchronization now uses the conflict planner, rather than uploading directly after each save. Overlapping sync requests share one operation; live gameplay defers sync until paused.
- Failed or incomplete native cloud listings no longer look like an empty cloud, preventing blind uploads when cloud files are not downloaded yet.
- Imported worlds tolerate null options and reject malformed recovery bags. Corrupt world-index entries cannot prevent saving another world. Null parental settings cannot prevent startup.
- Loading a saved world filters monsters against its difficulty, mode, monster setting and parental restriction before creating meshes.
- Swapping furnace ingredients with a full backpack preserves the previous ingredient and resets cooking progress.
- The horse-speed smoke check now compares the same unobstructed path for riding and walking, eliminating terrain-dependent failures.
- Recovery bags reuse one geometry instead of allocating a new geometry on every rebuild.
- Removed an unused smoke-test variable with an always-true predicate and an unused container parameter. No additional whole module was proven unused; runtime/native entry points remain.

## Recommended next work

1. Play a longer session with Gabriel on a real iPad, including save/relaunch, full inventory, sleep and recovery after fainting. Measure fps and thermal behaviour on the intended hardware.
2. Prioritize reliable cross-device cloud coordination and recovery before relying on iCloud for valuable worlds. Keep JSON exports as independent backups during real-device validation.
3. Extract persistence, UI and smoke checks from main.js without changing gameplay. This reduces the risk of future feature work.
4. Add building shapes and coloured wool, then block lighting. These directly enrich building and exploration. Implement tool wear only alongside clear child-friendly feedback.

Keep the offline, account-free scope. Multiplayer, advertising and purchases would add complexity without serving the current family game.

## Follow-up (same day)

Done since this review, each in its own commit and checked with the Node tests, the macOS smoke suite and XCUITest on iPad and iPhone simulators:

- **Building shapes and coloured wool:** ladders (climbable), fences, gates and trapdoors that open, signs with text, flower pots, seven dyes, eight wool colours and dyed sheep.
- **Block lighting:** light levels 0 to 15 for every cell. Sky light falls and spreads into openings; torches, lanterns, glowstone and furnaces are light sources. Faces are tinted by their light, edits relight a local box, and monsters spawn only at light 7 or below.
- **Tools, wear and missing content:**
  - Tools: axes, shovels and hoes in all four materials, plus shears, buckets, bottles and honey.
  - Tool wear: on Normal and Hard only, with a wear bar, a warning and a kind message.
  - Water: real water blocks that flow and dry up.
  - Generation 2 terrain: copper, underground lakes, plains wells and bee nests appear only in new worlds; older worlds keep their terrain.
  - Ambient animals: bees around their nests and fish in the sea.
- **Code organisation:**
  - `src/input/actions.js`: one action list for keyboard, touch and controllers.
  - `src/persistence.js`: storage, world snapshot and iCloud sync, with explicit hooks.
  - `src/ui/html.js`: small HTML pieces.
  - `src/dev/smoke-tests.js`: the in-game checks.
  - `main.js` went from about 5,900 to 4,900 lines. `render()` and `action()` stay in `main.js`, because moving them would need a large object of getters for little gain.

Second follow-up (same day), after a full check of the code:

- **Dead code:** an unused variable (`started`) removed, three exports that only their own module used made private, and the "use a bottle" hint for bee nests, which was written but never shown, now appears.
- **Backpack:** tap or drag one slot onto another; the same items join, anything else swaps; a picked stack can be split in half.
- **World pictures:** pausing keeps a small picture of the world for the world list. It stays on the device and is not synced.
- **Combat:** a shield anywhere in the hotbar stops half of a hit from the front and wears out; the bow is drawn while Build is held (mouse, touch button or trigger) and a longer draw shoots faster and harder.
- **Sounds:** footsteps that change with the ground (grass, sand, stone, wood, water) and a voice for every animal, when you use it and now and then nearby.
- **iPhone zoom bug:** text fields used 14 and 15 px text, so iOS zoomed the whole game in when one took focus (writing a sign, naming a dog or a world) and never zoomed back out; the top buttons ended up off screen. Fields now use 16 px and the page cannot be zoomed by the system.

Still outstanding:

- Lava: left out on purpose for a young player.
- Polish: the full map marker set, biome-specific animal distribution.

Bees and fish are ambient animals; they are not saved and do not breed.

Simulator WebGL needs the Mac's display awake: with the lid closed, the simulator loses its graphics context. Real devices, real controllers, real iCloud between devices, long sessions (fps and temperature) and signed distribution remain unvalidated.

Validation after the second follow-up: 97 Node tests and 169 smoke checks passed on macOS; XCUITest passed on iPad Pro 11-inch and iPhone 17e simulators (the iPhone run failed on the old code with the same zoom problem before the fix). ## Third follow-up (same day): lava and the rest of the brief

The whole v2 brief (`prompt-bieltructor-v2.md`) was checked again, item by item:

- **Lava (phases 2, 3 and 7):**
  - Generation 3 islands (new worlds) have lava pools on the floor of the deepest caves, never near the start; older worlds keep their terrain.
  - Lava is a light source (15), flows one block (slower than water) and cools into stone when water touches it. An empty bucket scoops a lava source.
  - On Normal and Hard it burns; on Peaceful and Easy it is harmless warm magma that pushes Biel out, as the brief asks. It never burns anything.
  - A backpack lost in lava is left at the last safe spot.
- **Blocks (phase 4):** campfire (light source; rain puts it out when nothing is above it, and a tap lights it again, as phase 2 asks), lantern, iron and gold blocks, red sand in deserts, and blue, white and yellow flowers (two of them make dyes).
- **Animals (phase 5):** each animal starts in the biome the brief gives it (plains, forest, snow).
- **HUD (phase 8):** "Icons instead of words" option for the game buttons. The map marker set (home, chests, tamed animals, plus monsters) was already complete.
- **Stickers (phase 9):** the dog sticker now needs the dog to be named, as the brief says.
- **Sound (phase 10):** silly monster voices (warning and hit), door, chest and furnace sounds, and slower, echoing cave music.

Still missing from the brief, all small:

- Blocks: cobblestone, and beds in colours.
- An "animals only friends" switch. Animals in this game can never be hurt, so the switch would change nothing.
- A shield that blocks on a button press. The shield works by itself from the hotbar.
- Despawning wild animals far away. Animals do not respawn, so their number stays small.
- Islands are 97 and 129 blocks wide instead of 96 and 128.

Validation after the third follow-up: 103 Node tests and 179 smoke checks passed on macOS, and XCUITest passed on iPad Pro 11-inch and iPhone 17e simulators. A staged capture on the Mac showed the new blocks and glowing lava. Nobody has played with lava on a real device yet, and the new sounds have not been listened to.

## Fourth follow-up (same day): the last items

- **Cobblestone:** made from stone (two give two); the furnace turns it back into stone.
- **Beds in colours:** six more beds (orange, yellow, green, blue, purple and black) from planks and coloured wool. They work like the first red bed for sleeping and as home.
- **"Animals only friends" switch:**
  - It is a world option, on by default for Peaceful and Easy, and on for every world saved before it existed.
  - When it is off, two hits catch a grown-up pig, cow, chicken or sheep that is not a friend. The animal vanishes in sparkles and leaves raw food, which the furnace cooks into better food.
  - Friends, babies and rabbits are never caught.
- **Shield held up:** holding Build with the shield in hand raises it, and a raised shield stops the whole hit from the front. A quick tap still opens doors.
- **Wild animals far away:** when there are more animals than the quality allows (after switching to Light), the farthest wild animal more than 40 blocks away leaves. Friends never do.
- **Island sizes:** these stay 97 and 129 blocks across instead of 96 and 128. Islands are symmetric around a centre block, like the 57-block small island the brief itself asks for, and changing the size would reshape existing islands.

Validation after the fourth follow-up: 107 Node tests and 182 smoke checks passed on macOS, and XCUITest passed on iPhone 17e and iPad Pro 11-inch simulators. The iPad needed a second run after its simulator failed to launch the test runner. Real devices are still untested.

## Fifth follow-up (same day): a line-by-line check of the brief

A new pass over every line of the brief found items that earlier reviews had missed or marked as done by mistake:

- **Worlds:**
  - Duplicating a world was missing; the first review wrongly listed it as done. It is now a ⧉ button in the world list that copies the world with its picture.
  - The new world screen now shows a small map of the island from the chosen seed and size. With no seed typed in, the island shown is the one that will be created.
- **Pause menu:** it now has Export, and Quit in the Mac app. The iPad and iPhone apps cannot quit themselves.
- **Animals:** the cow gives milk only into a bucket held in hand. A chicken sometimes drops a feather with its egg, and a feather makes twice the arrows.
- **Monsters:** the woolly spider attacks only at night and keeps away by day. Its reward is now string (used for a bow or a fishing rod), and Puffy leaves smoke dust (black dye), so every reward the brief lists is a recipe ingredient.
- **Falls:** falls now hurt only above four blocks, as the brief says.
- **iPhone:** the backpack and recipes, chests and the furnace fill the screen within the safe areas.
- **Performance:** finished particles (block crumbs, sparkles, hearts) go into a pool and are used again.
- **Testing:** the iOS run now also includes a large iPhone (17 Pro Max) next to the iPhone 17e and the iPad.

The animal limits stay at 28 and 40 (Light and Normal). The brief's 12 and 30 were given only as an example, and every world starts with 19 animals.

Validation after the fifth follow-up: 108 Node tests and 186 smoke checks passed on macOS. XCUITest passed on iPad Pro 11-inch, iPhone 17e and iPhone 17 Pro Max simulators. Physical devices remain untested.
